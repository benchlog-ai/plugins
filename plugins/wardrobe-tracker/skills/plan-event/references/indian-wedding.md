# Indian wedding functions — what to wear

Customs vary by region and family; the family's stated dress code or theme always overrides this guide. Ask when it is unclear. Use the menswear or womenswear table according to the user's `dress_style` in `profile/me` (ask once if unknown).

## Menswear

| Function | Typical mood | Formality (1–5) | Usual menswear | Color notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Haldi | Daytime, playful, turmeric gets on clothes | 2–3 | Simple cotton/linen kurta + pyjama or churidar, kolhapuris | Yellow, white, light pastels. Wear something you don't mind staining |
| Mehendi | Daytime/afternoon, relaxed, festive | 3 | Printed or light kurta, optional Nehru jacket | Greens, mint, pastels, bright florals |
| Sangeet / cocktail | Evening, dancing, glam | 3–4 | Embroidered kurta set, Nehru jacket, Indo-western, or a bandhgala | Jewel tones, metallic touches, black is often fine here; check the theme |
| Ceremony (pheras / muhurtham) | Traditional, often morning or night depending on region | 4–5 | Sherwani or silk kurta with jacket (North), silk veshti/mundu + shirt and angavastram (South), dhoti-kurta (East) | Rich festive colors: maroon, gold, ivory, royal blue, deep green. Ivory/gold is common in the South. Avoid plain all-black and all-white unless customary |
| Reception | Evening, more Western-leaning | 4–5 | Bandhgala, tuxedo or dark suit, or an Indo-western | Navy, black, midnight blue, wine, champagne |

## Womenswear

| Function | Formality (1–5) | Usual womenswear | Color notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Haldi | 2–3 | Cotton kurta set, simple sharara or a light saree; flat juttis or kolhapuris | Yellow, white, light pastels; nothing precious |
| Mehendi | 3 | Printed lehenga or skirt set, anarkali, or kurta with palazzos | Greens, mint, pastels, florals |
| Sangeet / cocktail | 3–4 | Embellished lehenga, sharara, Indo-western gown or a sequinned saree | Jewel tones, metallics; check the theme |
| Ceremony (pheras / muhurtham) | 4–5 | Silk saree (Kanjeevaram in the South), heavier lehenga or anarkali | Rich festive colors; avoid the bride's main color (often red) and plain all-black or all-white unless customary |
| Reception | 4–5 | Designer saree, lehenga, or an evening gown | Navy, wine, champagne, emerald, pastels with shimmer |

## Fabrics by season
- Hot or humid (most of India outside Dec–Feb, and the South year round): cotton, linen, chanderi, light silk blends.
- North India winter (Dec–Feb): raw silk, velvet, jacquard, wool-blend bandhgalas; plan a shawl for evenings.

## Accessories
- Menswear footwear: mojaris/juttis with kurtas and sherwanis; kolhapuris for haldi; leather shoes with bandhgala or suit at the reception. Optional: stole/dupatta, safa (turban, often provided by the family for the baraat), brooch, pocket square, a classic watch.
- Womenswear: juttis or block heels (lawns and long days); bangles, jhumkas or a statement necklace matched to the outfit's metal tone; a potli bag; a dupatta or shawl for evenings.

## Tailoring lead times
- Off-the-rack sets: buy any time; allow alteration time.
- Custom sherwani, bandhgala, lehenga or saree blouse: allow 3–6 weeks with a tailor in India; plan a fitting on arrival if time allows.
